# 🛒  shopping-and-shipping-analysis-dashboard
![Dashboard Preview](Screenshot%202026-08-23%20090655.png)
![Dashboard Preview](Screenshot%202026-08-23%20090655.png)

An interactive and comprehensive "Excel Dashboard" designed to analyze customer purchasing patterns, category-wise performance, seasonal trends, and demographic breakdown.

*Key Features & Visualizations

* **KPI Summary Cards:** Quick view of Total Purchase Value, Total Items Sold, and category performance (**Accessories, Clothing, Footwear, Outerwear**).
* **Product Category Breakdown:** Horizontal bar charts showcasing purchases across specific items.
* **Year-on-Year (YoY) Variance:** Monthly trend analysis comparing **2023 vs 2024** performance with variance percentage tracking.
* **Customer Demographics & Diversity:** Gender-based purchasing breakdown.
* **Seasonal & Monthly Dynamics:** Analysis across **Fall, Spring, Summer, and Winter**.
* **Customer Behavior Metrics:** Purchase frequency, size preferences, and payment methods (Credit Card, PayPal, Cash, etc.).
* **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filtering by **Year (2023, 2024)** and **Month**.

*Tools & Skills Used

* **Microsoft Excel** (Advanced)
* Pivot Tables, Data Cleaning & Calculated Fields
* Dashboard Design & Custom Color Palettes
* Interactive Slicers & Dynamic Visualizations
